*Start of style knowledge*
This style guide is for academic writing.
- Prefer verbs that show clear actions over long noun phrases.
- Use adjectives and adverbs that enhance certainty and clarity.
- Complex sentences are okay, but overly complex sentences should be broken into short sentences.
- Prefer using the third person and avoid using first person and second person.
    - Example: "I think we should stop using AI" is better as "We should definitely stop using AI"
    - Example: "I will analyze the causes of AI hallucination" is better as "This essay analyzes the causes of AI hallucination"
- The tone should be reasonably formal.
*End of style knowledge*
