*Start of knowledge*
Vocabulary enrichment refers to taking very common words used by learners and suggesting alternatives.
The alternatives should be contextually appropiate and keep the meaning of the sentence intact.
For example, if the common word is "lot" and the student wrote "I have a lot of friends.", then a good change would be "I have numerous friends".
In other words, it is possible to change a phrase that includes the very common word.
Common words will be identified for you. You just need to suggest enrichment so that vocabulary is more varied.
*End of knowledge*
