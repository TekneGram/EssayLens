The Advantages and Disadvantages of Uising Translation Apps

Translation apps are useful tools, but students should use them carefully. First, these apps can help students understand words and sentences quickly. For example, when students read an English website, they can check the meaning of difficult phrases. In addition, translation apps can help students communicate when they travel abroad. However, the translations are not always natural or correct. If students only copy the app’s answer, they may not improve their own English skills. Therefore, translation apps are helpful when they are used as learning tools, not as a complete answer.
