Why Learning English can Open New Opportunities
Daniel Parsons
Student Number: 345678
Date: May 3rd, 2026

Learning English can open many new opportunities for students in Japan. First, English can help students communicate with people from other countries. For example, they can make friends when they travel or meet international students at university. In addition, English is useful for finding information online because many websites and videos are in English. It can also help students in their future jobs, especially if they want to work for an international company. Learning English is sometimes difficult, but it gives students more choices in life. In this way, English is not only a school subject but also a key to the wider world.
