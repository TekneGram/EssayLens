The role of anime and manga in sharing Japanese culture

Anime and manga play an important role in sharing Japanese culture with the world. First, many people become interested in Japan after watching anime or reading manga. For example, they may want to learn Japanese, visit Tokyo, or try Japanese food. In addition, anime and manga often show parts of daily life, such as school events, festivals, and family relationships. These stories can help foreign audiences imagine life in Japan. Of course, anime and manga do not show all parts of Japanese culture, but they can be a friendly first step. In this way, anime and manga connect Japan with people around the world.
