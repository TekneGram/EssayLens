Studying abroad can change a person's way of thinking by giving them new experiences. First, students can learn that people have different customs and values. For example, they may notice that classroom discussions are more active in another country. In addition, students must solve daily problems by themselves, such as buying food or asking for directions. These experiences can make them more independent and confident. Studying abroad can also help students understand Japan better because they see their own culture from outside. In this way, studying abroad can change not only language skills but also a student’s view of the world.
