The advantages and disadvantages of studying in a group

Studying in a group has both advantages and disadvantages for learners. One advantage is that students can help each other understand difficult points. For example, one student may explain a grammar rule, while another may give useful examples or remember important information from class. In addition, group study can make learning feel more active and less lonely. However, some students may talk about unrelated topics instead of studying. Also, one or two students may do most of the work while others only listen. In the end, group study can be effective, but only when all members have a clear goal and participate seriously.
