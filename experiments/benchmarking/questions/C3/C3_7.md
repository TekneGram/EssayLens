The advantages and disadvantages of commuting to school by train

Commuting to school by train has both advantages and disadvantages for students. One advantage is that trains are usually reliable and easy to use in many parts of Japan. For example, students can often arrive at school on time because trains follow a regular schedule. In addition, train time can be used for small study tasks, such as reviewing vocabulary or reading class notes. Although commuting by train can be reliable and useful for short study activities, it also has disadvantages related to crowding and lost time. During rush hour, trains can be very crowded and stressful. Students may also spend a long time traveling every day, which can reduce their time for sleep, homework, or hobbies. Therefore, train commuting can be practical, but it can also make student life more tiring.
