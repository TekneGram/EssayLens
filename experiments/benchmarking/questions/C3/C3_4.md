The advantages and disadvantages of using AI for homework

Using AI for homework has both advantages and disadvantages for learners. One advantage is that AI can give quick support when students are confused. For example, it can explain grammar, suggest ideas, or show sample sentences. In addition, AI can help students notice mistakes and improve their writing before they submit homework. On the other hand, some students may copy AI answers without thinking carefully. This means they may finish the homework but fail to develop their own skills. As a result, AI can be a helpful learning tool, but it should not replace students’ own effort.
