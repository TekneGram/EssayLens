The advantages and disadvantages of learning English mainly through videos

Learning English mainly through videos has both advantages and disadvantages. One advantage is that videos can show real pronunciation, facial expressions, and situations. For example, students can watch conversations in restaurants, classrooms, or travel settings and learn how English is used naturally. In addition, videos are often enjoyable, so students may feel more motivated to continue studying. On the other hand, videos may not explain grammar or writing skills in a clear order. Students may also watch passively without practicing speaking or writing by themselves. In short, videos can be a powerful learning resource, but they should be combined with active practice and structured study.
