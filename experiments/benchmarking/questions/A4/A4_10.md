The problem of presentation anxiety among students

Presentations create anxiety and students might feel shaky, avoid eye contact and try to finish their presentations too quickly, meaning that their presentations are not good, so students need to overcome this anxiety as soon as they can. Many students worry that they will forget their words, make pronunciation mistakes, or be judged by their classmates. This fear can affect both their body and their mind. For example, their hands may shake, their voice may become quiet, or their thoughts may suddenly feel unclear. Because of this anxiety, some students avoid eye contact or read directly from their notes. Others may try to finish as quickly as possible instead of explaining their ideas carefully. In this way, presentation anxiety can stop students from showing what they really know.
