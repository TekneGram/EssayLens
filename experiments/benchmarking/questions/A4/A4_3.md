Why many students feel nervous when speaking English

Many students feel nervous when speaking English because they worry about making mistakes. One cause is that speaking happens quickly, so students do not have much time to think. Another cause is fear of pronunciation mistakes. For example, a student may know the right word but feel afraid to say it aloud. This nervousness can have several effects. Students may speak very quietly, use only short answers, or avoid speaking completely. As a result, they get fewer chances to improve their fluency. Therefore, students need a classroom where mistakes are seen as a normal part of learning.
