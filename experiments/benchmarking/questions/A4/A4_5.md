Studying English alone and studying English with a teacher

Studying English alone and studying English with a teacher are both useful, but they help students in different ways. Studying alone gives students freedom. They can choose their own textbook, study time, and speed. For example, a student can review vocabulary on the train or watch an English video at home. In contrast, studying with a teacher gives students guidance. A teacher can explain difficult points, correct mistakes, and give advice. However, teacher-led study may be less flexible because students must follow a class schedule. The best choice may be to use both methods, so students can enjoy freedom and receive support.
