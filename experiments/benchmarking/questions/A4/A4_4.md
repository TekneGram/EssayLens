How moving to a big city can change a student’s lifestyle

Moving to a big city can be tough. One cause of this change is transportation. In a big city, students often use trains and buses instead of cars or bicycles. This can make it easier to travel, but it can also make daily life feel crowded and busy. Another cause is the large number of shops, schools, events, and part-time jobs. Because of this, students may become more active and independent. However, they may also feel tired or lonely because city life can be fast and stressful. In this way, moving to a big city can bring both new opportunities and new pressures.
