The positive and negative effects of studying with YouTube

Studying with YouTube can be helpful, but it can also create some problems for students. One positive effect is that students can watch many free English lessons at any time. For example, they can learn pronunciation, grammar, or listening skills from different teachers around the world. YouTube videos can also be enjoyable because they use music, images, and real conversations. However, there are negative effects too. Students may become distracted by entertainment videos, comments, or advertisements. Also, not every teacher on YouTube gives clear or correct explanations. Therefore, YouTube can be a useful study tool, but students need to choose videos carefully and stay focused.
