Taking an English class online and taking one in person

Taking an English class online and taking one in person provide different kinds of learning experiences. Online classes are convenient because students can study from home and avoid travel time. They may also feel less nervous when speaking from their own room. In-person classes, however, make it easier to read facial expressions and react naturally during conversation practice. Students can also work with classmates more smoothly when they are in the same classroom. Some students like wearing fashionable clothes to school. In short, online classes offer convenience, while in-person classes often provide stronger face-to-face interaction.

Controlling idea: different kinds of learning experiences
