My first time giving a speech in English

My first time giving a speech in English taught me that preparation can reduce fear. Before the speech, I was worried that I would forget my words or make pronunciation mistakes. I practiced many times at home, first alone and then in front of my family. On the day of the speech, my hands were shaking, but I remembered the main points because I had practiced carefully. When I finished, my classmates clapped, and I felt relieved. English is spoken in many countries around the world. That experience showed me that careful preparation can make a difficult speaking task feel possible.

Controlling idea: preparation can reduce fear
