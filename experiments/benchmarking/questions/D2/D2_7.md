The day I helped a lost tourist

The day I helped a lost tourist showed me that even simple English can be useful in real life. I was walking near the station when a foreign visitor looked at a map with a worried face. He asked me how to get to a nearby temple, and at first I felt nervous about answering in English. I used short sentences and gestures to explain the direction. He smiled, said thank you, and walked toward the correct street. The station has many restaurants and shops on the second floor. After that experience, I felt more confident because I understood that communication does not have to be perfect to be helpful.

Controlling idea: even simple English can be useful in real life
