The problem of feeling left out in group work

Feeling left out in group work is a problem because it can stop students from participating and learning with others. In some groups, one or two confident students may make all the decisions. Quieter students may sit silently even when they have useful ideas. If this happens often, they may feel that their opinions do not matter. Group work is sometimes done with large sheets of paper and colored pens. This can make them less willing to speak in future activities. As a result, feeling left out can reduce both confidence and the value of group learning.

Controlling idea: feeling left out can stop students from participating and learning with others
