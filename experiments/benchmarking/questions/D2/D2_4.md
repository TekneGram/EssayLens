How moving to a new school can affect a student’s confidence

Moving to a new school can affect a student’s confidence by changing their social and academic environment. At first, students may feel nervous because they do not know their classmates or teachers. They may also worry about different school rules, classroom styles, or club activities. If they make friends quickly, they may begin to feel more comfortable and confident. However, if they feel left out, they may become quieter in class and avoid joining conversations. School uniforms are common in many Japanese junior high schools. As a result, moving to a new school can either build confidence or reduce it, depending on how well students adjust.

Controlling idea: moving to a new school changes a student’s social and academic environment, affecting confidence
