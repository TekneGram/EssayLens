A quiet morning in a convenience store

A quiet morning in a convenience store has a calm and ordinary atmosphere before the busy day begins. The lights are bright, and the shelves are neatly filled with rice balls, bread, drinks, and newspapers. A few customers enter slowly, buy breakfast, and leave without much conversation. Near the register, the sound of the scanner and coins is clear because the store is not crowded yet. Outside, the street is still quiet, and the morning air feels cool. Convenience stores often sell concert tickets and accept bill payments. In this peaceful moment, the store feels like a small part of daily life waking up.

Controlling idea: a calm and ordinary morning atmosphere before the busy day begins
