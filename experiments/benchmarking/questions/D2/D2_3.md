Why regular reading improves English writing

Regular reading improves English writing because it gives students repeated examples of how English sentences work. When students read often, they see useful vocabulary, grammar patterns, and sentence connections in context. For example, they may notice how writers use words such as “however,” “therefore,” and “in addition” to connect ideas. Reading also helps students understand how paragraphs are organized around one main point. Many popular novels have been made into movies. Over time, these examples can influence students’ own writing style and help them write more naturally. Therefore, regular reading supports writing by giving students models they can learn from.
