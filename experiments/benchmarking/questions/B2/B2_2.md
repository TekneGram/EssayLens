The positive and negative sides of living with grandparents

Living with grandparents can have both positive and negative sides for young people. On the positive side, grandparents can give lots of support at home. They may cook meals, listen to problems, or teach special family stories. This can make the home feel warm and safe. On the negative side, young people and grandparents may have different ideas about daily life. For example, they may disagree about clothes, phones, study, or free time. These differences can sometimes make small problems feel big. In short, living with grandparents can be very good, but it also needs patience and understanding.
