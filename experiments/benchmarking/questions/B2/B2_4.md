How joining a sports team can change a student’s daily habits

Joining a sports team can change a student’s daily habits in big ways. First, students need to practice at regular times, so their schedule becomes more organized. They may wake up earlier, finish homework faster, or sleep earlier at night. In addition, they may start to care more about food and health. For example, they may eat better meals because they need energy for practice. A sports team can also change their social life because they spend lots of time with teammates. These changes can be difficult at first, but they can make students’ lives more active. In this way, joining a sports team can create many new habits.
