A rainy afternoon in a Japanese shopping street

A rainy afternoon in a Japanese shopping street has a quiet but interesting feeling. The sky is gray, and small drops of rain fall on the street. People walk slowly with umbrellas, and their shoes make soft sounds on the wet ground. In front of the shops, bright signs and warm lights look special in the rain. Some people stop to buy hot snacks, bread, or tea. Others stand under a roof and wait for the rain to become weak. The street is not as busy as usual, but it still feels alive. A rainy shopping street shows a calm side of everyday life.
