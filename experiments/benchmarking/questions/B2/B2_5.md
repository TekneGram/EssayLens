Reading paper books and reading e-books

Reading paper books and reading e-books are both good ways to enjoy reading, but they feel different. Paper books are easy to hold, and many people like the feeling of turning real pages. They can also make reading feel calm and special. In contrast, e-books are very convenient because students can carry lots of books on one phone or tablet. E-books are also useful because readers can change the font size or use a dictionary quickly. However, reading on a screen may make some people tired. Both types of books have good points, so students can choose the one that fits their life best.
