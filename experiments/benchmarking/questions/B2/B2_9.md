The problem of feeling isolated in online classes

Feeling isolated in online classes is a big problem for some students. In a normal classroom, students can talk before class, ask small questions, and see their classmates’ faces. In online classes, these small chances are often missing. Many students keep their cameras off, so the class can feel quiet and empty. Because of this, students may feel that they are studying alone, even when many people are in the same online room. They may also find it hard to make friends or join group work naturally. Over time, this lonely feeling can make online learning less interesting. Therefore, isolation is one of the serious problems of online classes.
