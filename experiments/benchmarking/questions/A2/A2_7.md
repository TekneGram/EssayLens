At first, students may only write simple sentences, such as what they ate, where they went, or how they felt. After some time, they can add more details and connect their ideas more smoothly. For example, instead of writing, “I was tired,” a student might write, “I was tired because I studied late, so I went to bed early.” In this way, diary writing helps students practice vocabulary, grammar, and sentence connections naturally. It also helps them become less afraid of making mistakes. A diary may look like private writing, but it can build strong writing habits.
