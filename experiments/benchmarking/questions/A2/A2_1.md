First, students need time to remember new words and grammar. For example, they may understand a word today but forget it next week. In addition, speaking can feel difficult because students must think and respond quickly. Mistakes are a normal part of this process, but they can sometimes feel embarrassing. However, students usually improve little by little when they practice regularly. Therefore, patience is important because language learning is a long journey, not a quick race.
