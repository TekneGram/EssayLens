The problem of relying too much on translation apps

Relying too much on translation apps is a serious problem for language learners because it can weaken independent thinking. First, students may use an app before they try to understand a sentence by themselves. As a result, they may get an answer quickly, but they do not practice guessing meaning from context. In addition, students may copy translated sentences without checking whether they are natural or accurate. This means that they can produce English that looks complete but does not show their real ability. Over time, this habit can make students less confident when they must read, write, or speak without help. Therefore, overdependence on translation apps can stop students from developing stronger language skills.
