The problem of being too shy to ask questions in class

Being too shy to ask questions in class is a common problem because it can prevent students from getting the help they need. First, shy students may worry that their question is too simple or that classmates will judge them. Because of this, they may stay silent even when they do not understand the lesson. In addition, one small misunderstanding can lead to bigger problems later. For example, if a student does not understand one grammar point, the next activity may become much more difficult. Moreover, teachers may not notice the problem if students do not show confusion. Therefore, shyness in class is not just a personal feeling; it can directly affect learning and confidence.
