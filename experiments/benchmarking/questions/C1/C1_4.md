How getting a smartphone can change a junior high school student’s life

Getting a smartphone can change a junior high school student’s life in several important ways. First, communication becomes much easier because students can message friends and family at any time. Because of this, they may feel more connected to their classmates outside school. In addition, smartphones give students quick access to information, videos, games, and social media. However, this also means that their free time may change greatly. For example, a student who once spent evenings reading or playing outside may begin to spend more time looking at a screen. As a result, getting a smartphone can bring convenience and connection, but it can also change a young student’s habits very quickly.
