Why some students feel sleepy in afternoon classes

Some students feel sleepy in afternoon classes because their bodies and minds lose energy after lunch. First, students may become relaxed after eating, especially if they have had a large meal. As a result, it can be harder for them to concentrate on listening or reading. In addition, many students are already tired from morning classes, club activities, or part-time jobs. For example, a student who slept late the night before may feel especially sleepy during a quiet lecture. Moreover, warm classrooms and long explanations can make this sleepiness even stronger. Therefore, afternoon sleepiness is not only a problem of attitude; it often comes from a combination of food, tiredness, and classroom conditions.
