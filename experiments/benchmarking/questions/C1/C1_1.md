The positive and negative effects of using English learning apps

English learning apps can support students’ study, but they also have some limitations. First, these apps are convenient because students can practice vocabulary, listening, or grammar anywhere. For example, they can review words on the train or complete a short lesson before going to bed. In addition, many apps use points, levels, and reminders, so studying can feel more enjoyable and regular. However, apps do not always give enough deep practice, especially for speaking and writing. This means that students may remember answers in the app but still find real communication difficult. Therefore, English learning apps can be useful tools, but they work best when students combine them with other forms of study.
