A crowded summer festival in Japan

A crowded summer festival in Japan is lively, colorful, and full of energy. First, the streets are filled with people wearing yukata, walking slowly between food stalls. For example, visitors may buy yakisoba, shaved ice, or grilled corn while talking with friends. In addition, the sounds of music, laughter, and festival games create an exciting atmosphere. Bright lanterns also make the evening feel warm and special. However, the crowds can make it difficult to walk quickly or find a quiet place to rest. Even so, the busy movement is part of the festival’s charm. In this way, a crowded summer festival shows both the excitement and togetherness of Japanese summer culture.
