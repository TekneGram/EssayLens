The problem of feeling too afraid to make mistakes in English

Feeling too afraid to make mistakes in English is a serious problem because it can stop students from practicing. Students may worry that their classmates will laugh or think they are not good at English. They may choose silence instead of trying to speak. In addition, fear of mistakes can make students use only very simple words, even when they know more interesting expressions. A student may want to explain an opinion but give only a short answer because they are afraid of grammar errors. Over time, this fear can reduce confidence and fluency. Being too afraid of mistakes can make English learning slower and less active.
