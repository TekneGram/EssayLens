Why some students lose confidence when speaking English

Some students lose confidence when speaking English because they focus too much on mistakes. They may worry about grammar, pronunciation, or choosing the wrong word. When they speak in front of classmates, they may feel that everyone is judging them. This fear can make them speak more quietly or give only short answers. As a result, they get fewer chances to practice speaking naturally. Over time, less practice can lead to even lower confidence. Therefore, fear of mistakes can create a cycle that makes speaking English harder.
