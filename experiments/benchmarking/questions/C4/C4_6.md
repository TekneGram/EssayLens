How making international friends can improve language motivation

Making international friends can improve language motivation because students have a real reason to communicate. Instead of studying English only for tests, they can use it to share opinions, ask questions, and learn about another person’s life. They may want to understand messages, join conversations, or explain Japanese culture clearly. It makes English feel more meaningful. Because communication has a personal goal, students may study vocabulary and pronunciation with more interest. They may also become less afraid of making small mistakes. In this way, international friendships can turn English from a school subject into a useful communication tool.
