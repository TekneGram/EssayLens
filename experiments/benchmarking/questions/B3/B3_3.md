Why some students become more confident after joining a club

Some students become more confident after joining a club because they get lots of chances to try new things. At first, they may feel shy or nervous around new people. However, they slowly learn how to talk with other members and join group activities. For example, a student in a music club may practice with others and then perform at a school event. This kind of experience can make the student feel proud. Also, club members often give support, so students do not feel alone. As a result, joining a club can help students believe in themselves more.
