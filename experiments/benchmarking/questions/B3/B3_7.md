A bright spring day on a university campus

A bright spring day on a university campus feels fresh, warm, and full of life. The trees have new green leaves, and some flowers add soft colors near the paths. Students walk between buildings, carrying bags and talking with friends. Some students sit on benches and eat lunch in the sun. Others stand near the gate and check their phones before class. The air feels light, and the campus looks more open than in winter. Even ordinary buildings seem nicer in the bright weather. A spring day can make university life feel new and hopeful.
