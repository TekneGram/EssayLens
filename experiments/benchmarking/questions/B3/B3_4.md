How a long commute can affect a student’s daily life

A long commute can affect a student’s daily life in many big ways. First, students may lose lots of time every morning and evening. If they spend two hours on trains or buses each day, they have less time for homework, sleep, and hobbies. This can make them feel tired before school even starts. In addition, crowded trains can be stressful, especially during rush hour. Because students arrive home late, they may eat dinner late and go to bed late. Therefore, a long commute can make a student’s whole day more difficult and tiring.
