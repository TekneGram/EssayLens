The positive and negative effects of studying in a café

Studying in a café has both good points and bad points for students. One good point is that cafés can feel comfortable and interesting. Students can enjoy a drink, sit in a nice place, and do their work in a different space from home. Also, a little background noise may help some students feel less lonely. However, cafés can also have bad points. They may be too noisy, too crowded, or too expensive for long study sessions. Because of this, studying in a café can be useful sometimes, but it is not always the best place for serious study.
