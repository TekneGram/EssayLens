The positive and negative sides of using part-time job money freely

Using part-time job money freely has both good and bad sides for students. On the good side, students can enjoy their own money and feel more independent. For example, they can buy clothes, go out with friends, or pay for hobbies without always asking their parents. This can make students feel happy and more adult. On the bad side, some students may spend too much money on small things. They may not have enough money later for important things, such as textbooks or transportation. In short, using job money freely can feel good, but it can also cause money problems if students are not careful.
