Festivals help people feel connected to their community by bringing them together for a shared experience. At a local festival, children, adults, and older people can enjoy the same event. Some people carry a mikoshi, some sell food, and others watch dances or music. These activities create chances for people to talk and cooperate. In addition, festivals often remind people of their town’s history and traditions. Even people who are usually busy can feel that they belong to the same place. In this way, festivals are not only fun events; they also strengthen community life.
