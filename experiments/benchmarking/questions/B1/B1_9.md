The positive and negative effects of using AI tools for English study

Using AI tools for English study has both good points and bad points. One good point is that students can get help very quickly. For example, they can ask AI about grammar, vocabulary, or the meaning of a sentence. AI can also give lots of examples, so students can see how English is used. However, there are also some bad points. Students may use AI too much and stop thinking by themselves. Sometimes AI may also give strange or wrong answers. Therefore, AI can be a good study partner, but students need to use it carefully.
