A lively school sports day

A lively school sports day is full of energy, teamwork, and excitement. At first, the school ground becomes colorful with class flags, uniforms, and event signs. Then, students begin to run races, pull ropes, and cheer for their classmates. For example, during a relay race, the crowd often becomes louder as the final runners get closer to the finish line. In addition, teachers and parents add to the warm atmosphere by clapping, taking photos, and encouraging students. However, sports day is not only about winning. It also gives students a chance to cooperate, support each other, and feel proud of their class. Therefore, a lively sports day can become one of the most memorable events in school life.
