Learning English from textbooks and learning English from videos

Learning English from textbooks and learning English from videos can both be useful, but they support different kinds of learning. Textbooks are helpful because they usually explain grammar, vocabulary, and reading skills in an organized way. For example, students can study one unit at a time and review important points before a test. In contrast, videos often show how English sounds and feels in real communication. In addition, videos can include facial expressions, gestures, and natural pronunciation. However, videos may not always explain language points clearly or step by step. Therefore, textbooks are useful for structure, while videos are useful for listening and real-life language.
