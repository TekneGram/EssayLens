*Start of linguistic coherence knowledge*
Coherence is achieved through a number of linguistic strategies between sentences.

## Examples
- contrasts such as *however*, *in contrast*, *while* and *whereas* can help contrast points between sentences.
- additions such as *furthermore*, *in addition*, and *moreover* between sentences help add more information.
- elaborations such as *for example*, *in fact* and *such as* allow elaboration between sentences.
- cause-effect phrases like *as a result of this* and *due to* show cause and effect relationships between sentences.
- showing reasons with expressions like *because of*, *because* and *since* between sentences.

*End of linguistic coherence knowledge*
