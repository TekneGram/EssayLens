*Start of knowledge*
Pronouns should have a clear reference.
Pronouns should match in number, being plural or singular.
It is common for novice writers to mix up *I*, *you*, *we*, *they*, *their*, *your*, *our*, *it* and others when writing.
For example, novice writers sometimes writes *it* when they mean *they*, or they write *its* when they mean *their*.
Learners are focusing on academic writing, where the use of first person *I* and second person *you* tends to be avoided.
The most important thing is that writers should be consistent in their use of pronouns.
