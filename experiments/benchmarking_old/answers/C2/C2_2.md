The positive and negative sides of using social media to practice English

Using social media to practice English has both positive and negative sides for language learners. On the positive side, students can read real English posts, watch short videos, and communicate with people from different countries. For instance, they may follow English teachers, comment on simple posts, or learn useful expressions from daily conversations. In addition, social media can make English feel more connected to real life rather than only to school tests. However, it can also waste time because students may move from English content to entertainment content very easily. This means that practice time can quickly become scrolling time. In short, social media can support English learning, but only when students use it with a clear purpose.
