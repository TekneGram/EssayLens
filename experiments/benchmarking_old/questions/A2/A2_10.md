A good presentation is not only about speaking English perfectly. It is also about organizing information, choosing important points, and helping listeners understand. For example, students can begin with a clear introduction, explain two or three main ideas, and end with a strong conclusion. These skills are useful for group projects at university and meetings in future jobs. Presentations can also build confidence because students learn to speak in front of others step by step. In the end, presentation skills help students turn their ideas into messages that other people can understand.
