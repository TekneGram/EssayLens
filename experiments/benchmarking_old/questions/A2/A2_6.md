A short video about breakfast in Korea, a photo of a festival in Mexico, or a post about school life in France can make another culture feel more real. These examples are useful because culture is not only found in textbooks. It is also found in food, clothes, music, family habits, and ways of speaking. In addition, people can follow creators who explain their own culture in simple and personal ways. However, users should remember that one post does not show a whole country or all its people. Social media is a helpful window to other cultures, but we need to look through it thoughtfully.
