Imagine a tourist standing in a busy station and looking confused. The person may not understand the signs, know which train to take, or feel confident enough to ask in Japanese. In this situation, even simple English can be useful. For example, saying “This train goes to Kyoto” or “The ticket office is over there” can solve a real problem. Helping tourists also gives Japanese students a chance to use English outside the classroom. Therefore, helping visitors is more than kindness; it is a real form of international communication.
