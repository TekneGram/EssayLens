Without breakfast, students may feel tired before their classes even begin. They may also find it harder to listen carefully or remember new information. A simple meal, such as rice and miso soup, toast and eggs, or fruit and yogurt, can give students useful energy. This does not mean breakfast has to be large or expensive. What matters most is eating something that supports concentration and health. For busy students, breakfast is not just a morning habit; it is preparation for a better day.
