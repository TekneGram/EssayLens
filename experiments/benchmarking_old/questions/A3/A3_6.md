Alone, students may feel nervous when they practice speaking. With classmates, however, they can ask questions, share ideas, and try conversations together. For example, two students can practice ordering food, giving opinions, or preparing a short presentation. Group learning also helps students notice different ways to express the same idea. Sometimes classmates make mistakes, but those mistakes can become useful learning chances for everyone. Therefore, learning English with others can help students build both language skills and confidence.
