After a long time at a desk, the mind becomes tired. When this happens, students may read slowly, forget information, or feel frustrated. A short break can refresh the brain and make the next study period more effective. For example, students can stand up, stretch, drink water, or walk for a few minutes. The key point is to return to studying after the break, not to spend an hour on social media. Used wisely, breaks are not an escape from study; they are a part of successful study.
