Convenience stores make life in Japan easier because they offer many useful services in one place. First, people can buy food, drinks, and daily items at almost any time. For example, a busy student can buy lunch before class or dinner after a part-time job. In addition, convenience stores have services such as ATMs, ticket machines, and package delivery. These services save time because people do not need to visit many different places. Convenience stores are also easy to find in most cities and towns. In short, convenience stores support people’s daily lives in simple but important ways.
