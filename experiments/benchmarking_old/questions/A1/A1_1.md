Clear organization is more important than complex vocabulary in academic writing. Many students believe that academic writing requires complex vocabulary because they want to sound intelligent. As a result, they often spend too much time choosing difficult words. However, this focus can make their writing unclear and hard to follow. When sentences become complicated, readers may lose track of the main idea. In contrast, writing with clear structure helps readers understand ideas more easily. Therefore, even simple words can be effective if ideas are logically organized.
