Smartphones affect students' study habits in both helpful and harmful ways. First, smartphones can support learning because students can use dictionaries, videos, and study apps. For example, they can review English vocabulary on the train. In addition, students can contact classmates easily when they need help with homework. However, smartphones can also distract students with games, messages, and social media. If students check their phones too often, they may lose focus and study less deeply. Therefore, smartphones can be powerful study tools, but students need good self-control to use them well.
