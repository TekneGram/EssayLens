Why Japanese school lunches are useful for children's health.

Japanese school lunches are useful for children's health because they teach good eating habits. First, the meals usually include rice or bread, vegetables, soup, and protein. This balance helps children learn what a healthy meal looks like. In addition, students often eat the same lunch together, so they can try foods they do not usually eat at home. School lunches also teach students about food culture and local ingredients. For example, some schools use vegetables from nearby farms. Therefore, Japanese school lunches support children’s health and help them learn about food in daily life.
