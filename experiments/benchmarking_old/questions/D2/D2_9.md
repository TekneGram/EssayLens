The problem of losing motivation after a bad test score

Losing motivation after a bad test score is a serious problem because it can make students stop trying. When students receive a low score, they may feel disappointed or embarrassed. They may also begin to think that they are not good at the subject. This feeling can be especially strong if they studied hard but did not get the result they wanted. Some tests are printed on both sides of the paper. Over time, one bad score can affect future study habits, classroom participation, and confidence. Therefore, losing motivation after a bad test score can turn one disappointing result into a longer learning problem.
