Taking online English lessons has both advantages and disadvantages for students. One advantage is convenience. Students can take lessons at home, so they do not need to travel to a language school. In addition, online lessons can connect students with teachers from different countries, which gives them more chances to hear different accents and communication styles. Despite this, some students may find it harder to communicate naturally through a screen. They may also become distracted by their phone, family members, or other things at home. In short, online English lessons can support language learning, but the learning environment needs to be managed carefully.
