The problem of forgetting English vocabulary after tests

Forgetting English vocabulary after tests is a common problem for many students. Before a test, students may remember lots of words for a short time. They may study hard, repeat the words many times, and get a good score. However, after the test, they often stop using those words. Because of this, the words slowly disappear from their memory. This problem is serious because vocabulary is not only for tests; it is also needed for reading, listening, speaking, and writing. When students forget words again and again, they may feel that English study never really stays in their mind. In this way, short-term memorization can become a big problem for long-term language learning.
