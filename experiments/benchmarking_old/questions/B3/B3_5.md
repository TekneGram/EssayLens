Taking notes by hand and taking notes on a computer

Taking notes by hand and taking notes on a computer are both useful, but they are good for different reasons. Taking notes by hand can help students think more carefully because they cannot write every word. They need to choose the important ideas and write them simply. This can make learning deeper. In contrast, taking notes on a computer is faster and easier to organize. Students can move sentences, add pictures, and search for words later. However, computers can also bring distractions, such as messages or websites. In the end, both methods can be good, but students should choose the one that helps them focus best.
