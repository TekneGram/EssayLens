The problem of not having enough time to study English

Not having enough time to study English is a common problem for busy students. Many students already have a full schedule with school, homework, club activities, and part-time jobs. English study may be pushed to the end of the day, when students are already tired. In addition, language learning needs regular practice, not only a long study session before a test. Students who cannot find small amounts of time may forget vocabulary and lose confidence. Even if they want to improve speaking or listening, they may not have enough quiet time to practice. A lack of time can make English learning feel difficult, even for students who have motivation.
