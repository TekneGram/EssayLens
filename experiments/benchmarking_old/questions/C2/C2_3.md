Why some students stop doing homework regularly

Some students stop doing homework regularly because school life can become too busy and stressful. Students may have many classes, club activities, and part-time jobs in the same week. They may feel too tired to start their homework after they get home. In addition, if homework feels too difficult, students may avoid it because they do not know where to begin. A student who cannot understand the first question may decide to do something easier instead. Over time, missing one homework task can become a habit of missing many tasks. Students often stop doing homework not because they are lazy, but because tiredness, difficulty, and bad habits build up together.
