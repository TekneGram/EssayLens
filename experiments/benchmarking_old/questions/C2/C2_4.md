How moving away from home can change a university student’s life

Moving away from home can change a university student’s life by increasing both freedom and responsibility. Students can make more decisions by themselves, such as what to eat, when to study, and how to spend their free time. They may feel more independent than before. In addition, they must take care of daily tasks that their family may have done for them in the past. They need to cook, clean, do laundry, and manage money. However, this new independence can sometimes feel lonely or stressful. Moving away from home can help students grow, but it also teaches them how much support they received before.
