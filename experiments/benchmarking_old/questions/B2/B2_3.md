Why some students lose motivation during a long semester

Some students lose motivation during a long semester because they become tired and bored. At the beginning, classes may feel new and interesting. Students may want to study hard and do well. However, after many weeks, they may have lots of homework, tests, and club activities. Because of this, their energy can go down. If they do not see quick results, they may also feel that studying is not useful. This can make them stop trying their best. Therefore, a long semester can change students’ feelings from excitement to tiredness.
