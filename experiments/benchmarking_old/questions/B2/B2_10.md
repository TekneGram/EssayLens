The problem of food waste in school cafeterias

Food waste in school cafeterias is a serious problem because lots of good food is thrown away every day. Some students take more food than they can eat. Others leave food because they do not like the taste or because they are not very hungry. This waste is not only about food; it is also about money, time, and work. For example, cooks spend many hours preparing meals, but some of that food goes straight into the trash. Food waste can also make students think less carefully about the value of food. When this happens every day, a small problem becomes a big one. In short, food waste in school cafeterias shows a bad gap between what people prepare and what people actually eat.
