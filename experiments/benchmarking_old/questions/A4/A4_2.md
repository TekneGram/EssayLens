The positive and negative sides of wearing school uniforms

Wearing school uniforms is an important issue to think about. On the positive side, uniforms can make students feel equal because everyone wears the same clothes. They can also save time in the morning because students do not need to choose an outfit. In addition, uniforms can create a strong school identity. On the negative side, some students may feel that uniforms reduce their freedom. They may want to show their personality through their clothes. Uniforms can also be uncomfortable in very hot or cold weather. In short, school uniforms can support equality and school spirit, but they may also limit students’ personal expression.
