Why many students feel tired during exam season

Many students feel tired during exam season because their study load suddenly becomes heavier. They may review many subjects, complete extra assignments, and worry about their scores. They may also sleep less because they try to study late at night. This pressure affects both their body and their mind. For example, they may feel sleepy in class, lose concentration, or become easily irritated. If the tiredness continues, studying can become less effective even when they spend many hours at their desk. Therefore, exam season can make students tired because it combines long study time with emotional stress.
