How regular exercise can affect students’ concentration

Regular exercise can affect students’ concentration by improving their energy and mood. When students move their bodies, they can reduce stress and feel more awake. Exercise can also help them sleep better, which is important for clear thinking the next day. This can make it easier for students to focus during lessons and homework. For example, a student who takes a short walk after school may return to study with a fresher mind. Regular exercise does not replace studying, but it can support the mental condition needed for study. Therefore, physical activity can have a positive effect on learning.
