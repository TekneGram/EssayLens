How receiving positive feedback can affect a student’s motivation

Receiving positive feedback can affect a student’s motivation by helping them notice their progress. A student may work hard but feel unsure about whether they are improving. When a teacher says that their explanation is clearer or their pronunciation is better, the student can see that their effort has meaning. It can encourage the student to continue studying. It may also make them more willing to try difficult tasks because they believe improvement is possible. Over time, positive feedback can build confidence as well as motivation. In short, encouragement can help students connect effort with growth.
