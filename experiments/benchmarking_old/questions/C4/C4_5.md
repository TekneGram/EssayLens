Why living far from school can affect study habits

Living far from school can affect study habits because commuting takes time and energy. A student who travels a long distance may need to wake up early and return home late. After a crowded train ride or a long bus trip, the student may feel too tired to start homework immediately. This situation can reduce the time available for review, reading, and test preparation. It may also make students study only at night, when their concentration is weaker. Over time, the long commute can make regular study more difficult to maintain. Therefore, distance from school can strongly influence how and when students study.
