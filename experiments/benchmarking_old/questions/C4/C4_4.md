How joining a volunteer activity can change a student’s view of society

Joining a volunteer activity can change a student’s view of society by showing them problems they may not usually notice. For example, students may help older people, clean a local area, or support children at an event. Through these activities, they can meet people with different needs and life experiences. They may also see how small actions can support a community. This can make students think more seriously about social responsibility. It can also help them understand that society depends on cooperation, not only on government or schools. As a result, volunteering can help students become more aware and thoughtful citizens.
