Why some students become distracted during online lessons

Some students become distracted during online lessons because their learning environment is not well controlled. They may take the lesson in their bedroom, near a phone, or beside a game console. Family members, notifications, and other websites can also interrupt their attention. This environment makes it easier to stop listening to the teacher. Once students lose focus, they may miss important explanations or instructions. They may then feel confused during later activities because they did not understand the earlier part of the lesson. Therefore, online distractions can quickly affect both attention and understanding.
