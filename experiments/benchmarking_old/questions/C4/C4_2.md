How using a planner can improve a student’s daily routine

Using a planner can improve a student’s daily routine by making tasks clearer. Students often have homework, tests, club activities, and part-time jobs in the same week. Without a clear plan, they may forget important tasks or start them too late. A planner helps students see what they need to do and when they need to do it. It can reduce stress because students do not have to keep every task in their head. It can also help them use small amounts of free time more effectively. In this way, using a planner can make daily life more organized and calm.
