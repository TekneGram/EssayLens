Why too much screen time can change sleep habits

Too much screen time can change sleep habits because it keeps the mind active late at night. Students may watch videos, play games, or check social media even after they plan to sleep. One more video or message can easily become thirty more minutes of screen use. This delay can make students go to bed later than they intended. In addition, using a bright screen before sleep may make it harder to relax. If this happens often, students may wake up tired and struggle to concentrate the next day. Therefore, too much screen time can slowly damage a healthy sleep routine.
