Studying in the morning and studying at night

Studying in the morning and studying at night can both be effective, but they suit different kinds of students. In the morning, students may feel fresh after sleeping, so they can often concentrate well on difficult tasks. For example, morning study may be useful for reading, writing, or solving problems. In addition, there are usually fewer messages and distractions early in the day. On the other hand, studying at night can be helpful for students who feel more awake later. However, night study can become a problem if it reduces sleep time. Therefore, the better study time depends on each student’s energy, schedule, and ability to stay focused.
