The academic paragraphs you will read are written by second language learners.
They contain simple vocabulary such as "lots of" or "big".
We are giving feedback on ways to enhance the vocabulary so that it is more precise.

Imagine a student has written "AI is good for students because it helps them study."
An enhanced version would be "AI can be beneficial for students because it support their learning."
An even more enhanced version is "AI can be beneficial for students because it provides personalized support and facilitates independent study."

"Good" became "beneficial", "helps" became "support", "them study" became "their learning".
None of the replacements are unnatural. This means the context surrounding a word is important.
Any enhancements will help students to sound more academic and more natural in their academic style paragraph writing.

Vocabulary in academic writing tries to be more precise, formal, cautious, topic-appropriate and logically connected to claims, causes, effects, evidence and evaluation.
However, pedagogically, we should keep close the student's intended meaning. Therefore, any alternative vocabulary suggestions that do not fit the intended meaning would probably not help the student.
