# Overview
This overview provides a commentary on expected *issues* in the synthetic writing.
Note that the synthetic writing was written by the researcher based on experience of reading thousands of students' work and getting insights into the way they write and manage the writing process. None of the examples were written by AI.

## w1
### Organization
- Topic: Smartphones
- Controlling idea: positive effects
- Supporting points: Watch YouTube on the train
- Conclusion: positive effect

### Assessment
- Topic sentence is structurally sound but vague. The reader might want to know what kind of effects.
- There is only one supporting point despite the controlling idea suggesting many reasons.
- The supporting point only vaguely develops the controlling idea because "learning history" and "passing the journey time" seem like unrelated concepts mixed together.
- There paragraph seems to lack unity again because "learning history" and "passing the journey time" do not really work together to show positive effects on life.
- Coherence is lacking. "Learning history" and "passing the journey time" could be two separate points, but a lack of coherence makes this difficult to see.
- The example of learning history could be made into the controlling idea to make the paragraph more interesting.
- Overall, this is very weak writing.

## w2
### Organization
- Topic: Kyoto
- Controlling idea: many sightseeing places
- Supporting points: overcrowding; visiting other cities
- Conclusion: government should promote other cities

### Assessment
- Topic sentence is too general. The supporting sentences mention overcrowding, so this should probably be included in the controlling idea.
- The point about getting on buses should be framed as an example that can explain why overcrowding is a problem.
- Coherence is lacking, so sentences do not flow together smoothly.
- There are stylistic issues: use of "you" versus "they" makes understand the addressee in the paragraph difficult, grammar errors like "there is many Japanese culture"
- Overall, this is very weak writing.

## w3
### Organization
- Topic: Challenge of using AI
- Controlling idea: ethical use
- Supporting points: the ethical issue of letting AI do all the work such a generating an essay
- Conclusion: over-reliance on AI prevents learning

### Assessment
- Topic sentence is strong and well focused.
- Supporting sentences only develop one issue focusing on the specific example of having AI generate essays. The sentences contrast this with more ethical ways og using AI to emphasize the point.
- Coherence is reasonable: use of "However", "This" and "But" at the start of sentences help glue the sentences together and help them flow.
- However, the second sentence seems out of place and could benefit from some explanation.
- Overall, this is reasonable writing, but there is room to develop this.

## w4
### Organization
- Topic: Issues of using AI
- Controlling idea: ethical use
- Supporting points: honesty, over-reliance, fact-checking.
- Conclusion: Improving AI literacy should ensure that students are aware of these ethical uses.

### Assessment
- Topic sentence is strong and well focused.
- Supporting sentences develop three points in detail.
- Coherence is excellent. The transitions between each point are clearly sequenced, and each point is developed with examples and explanations.
- The conclusion sentence wraps up the main points using summary words and emphasizes the ethical challenge, which creates a clear take-away message for the reader.
- Stylistically, the writer has used a wide range of sentence structures and vocabulary.
- Overall, this is a very strong paragraph, and feedback should be focused on praising the writer's technique. There is no need to offer advice for improving anything.
