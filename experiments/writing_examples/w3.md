One of the biggest challenges students face with AI is learning how to use it ethically. Students often write essays. However, these days, students can easily ask AI to generate their essay for them. This is a big problem because when the teacher looks at the essay, the teacher gives feedback that is for AI. This does not help the student learn because the student did not write the essay. I think it is okay if students use AI to get new ideas and information. It is also okay if students use AI to check their work. But, they should not let AI do all their work for them. If AI does everything, then the student will not be able to do anything.
