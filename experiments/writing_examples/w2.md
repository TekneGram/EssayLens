Kyoto has many sightseeing places. Many tourists visit Kyoto because they want to see famous temples. However, the temples and shrines get too crowded. So people who live in Kyoto cannot get on the bus. When they go to work it is so tough. I think the Japanese government should make tourists go to other places, like outside Kyoto or in Osaka where we can eat delicious takoyaki. There is many Japanese culture you can experience in other cities like Osaka. So I think the government should make tourists go to other places to solve Kyoto's crowd problem.
