## Citations
Citations in text come in two forms:
- Narrative citation. Example: According to Johns (2003), ... . Example: Joshua (2024) argued that ...
- Parenthetical citation. Example: We know that this is true (Johns, 2003)

## References
References are always written at the end of the document. They should always correspond with at least one in-text citation.
One reference can correspond to many in-text citations; in other words, the source might be cited more than once in the text.
Examples:
Johns, K. R. (2003). The truth we know. Journal of International Philosophy, 14(3), pp. 18-54.
Joshua, M. (2024). The case for flu vaccines. Paramedical Journal, Vol 2(3).
