*Start of citations knowledge*
This is about in-text citations.
Citations refer to ideas or work from other people and indicate that the student is trying to incorporate research.
In text citations often refer to named individuals or named entities. They often contain a year in brackets.

*Examples*
- According to Jones (2024), ...
- Davies (2026) argues ...
- The Royal Institute (2023) noted that ...
- There are many issues with writing (Jones, 2024)
- We often see this problem getting worse (Berringer, 2019)
- And other have argued otherwise (OpenAI, 2025)

*End of citations knowledge*
