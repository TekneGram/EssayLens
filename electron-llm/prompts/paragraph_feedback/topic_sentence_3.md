# Knowledge about topic sentence
A general topic sentence: It does not focus the paragraph on a specific point. We cannot easily judge what ideas the paragraph will develop.
A topic sentence that is too specific: The topic sentence has too many details. We would expect these details to appear in the supporting sentences, such as reasons, examples and definitions.
A perfect topic sentence: The controlling idea allows the reader to imagine what details will appear in the supporting sentences.
Note that "for many reasons" and "a number of issues", and any kind of enumeration is considered a good topic sentence. Don't ask me why!
